# UBC MDS Cheatsheets (2025–2026)

Cheatsheets for UBC MDS courses, for anyone who couldn’t make their own.

These reflect **my learning needs at the time I took each quiz**, so they may not cover everything. You’re welcome to:

- **Use** them as-is  
- **Copy and adapt** them for your own use  
- **Extend** them with extra material as you need  

**Missing content?** Please open an **issue**. I’ll try to update before that date. Once a quiz is over, I generally won’t update that cheatsheet anymore.

*Good luck with your quizzes.*
